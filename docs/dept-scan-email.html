A faster way to do Department Check

Now live in the Scanner App — no Save button, just scan and go.

There is a new Dept Scan screen for Department Check. It is much quicker than the old way. The old way still works exactly as before — this is simply a faster option.

Finding it

Open the Scanner App as normal and tap Dept Scan in the menu, beside HO Tasks. On a handheld it is in the bar along the bottom of the screen.

Not showing? Close the app completely and open it again.

The screen

1 2 3 4 5 6
  • 1
    Back
    Returns you to HO Tasks. The normal menus are hidden here to keep the screen clear.
  • 2
    Counter
    How many you have scanned this session.
  • 3
    Department
    The answer you are looking for. It stays on screen until the next scan, so you can look at the shelf and back.
  • 4
    Scan box
    You never need to tap this. Just pull the trigger.
  • 5
    Undo Last Scan
    Scanned something by mistake? One tap removes it.
  • 6
    Your scans
    Everything so far, newest at the top. Swipe the table sideways for price, supplier and more.

Scanning

  1. Pull the trigger. The barcode goes in on its own — no need to tap anything first.
  2. It saves by itself and beeps. There is no Save button on this screen.
  3. Go straight to the next product. That is the whole loop.
Tap the screen once when you open it.
That switches the sounds on, so you can hear the beeps without looking at the screen.

Good to know

Same item twice?Two low beeps and Already scanned. It is not saved twice.
Scanned by mistake?Tap the red Undo Last Scan.
No signal?Keep scanning. The screen shows how many are waiting and they send themselves once you are back in range. Nothing is lost.
Barcode not known?It still saves and says head office will add it. Nothing for you to do.
Colours look different?Normal — the screen follows your device's light or dark setting.
Finished?Tap ← Back at the top left.
