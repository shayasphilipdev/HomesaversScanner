Deleting Department Check & Price Check records

How to permanently remove Department Check (J) and Price Check (K) records from Reports — including everything that matches your filter, not just the page on screen.

⚠️

Delete is permanent. Deleted records are gone from the database for good — there is no undo.

Where: Reports → HO records Applies to: Department Check & Price Check only Who: every logged-in user (stores limited to their own stores)
  1. Open Reports and set your filters

    Go to Reports → HO records. Choose your From / To dates, the Stores, and a Status if you want (e.g. Pending). These decide exactly which records get deleted.

  2. Pick the task types, then run the report

    Open Task Types and tick Price Check and Department Check (untick everything else), press Done, then click Run report.

  3. Click “Delete all matching J/K”

    In the results header, click the red 🗑 Delete all matching J/K button. This targets every matching record across all pages — not only the rows currently loaded.

  4. Check the confirmation box carefully

    A red box shows how many records will be deleted and the exact date range (“Deleting records dated…”). Make sure the count and dates are what you expect.

  5. Confirm, then let it finish

    Click Delete … permanently to go ahead, or Cancel to stop. Large deletes are removed in batches and can take up to a minute — leave the page open until it finishes.

💡

Keep the app fast. Once a Department Check or Price Check has been completed, please delete its records from the app. Removing finished checks keeps the database lean and reduces load, so the app stays quick and responsive for every store.

•To delete a single record instead, use the red 🗑 Delete button on that record’s row — the same confirmation box appears.
•Only Department Check and Price Check can be deleted this way. Other task types keep the normal Head Office review flow.
